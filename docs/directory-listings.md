# Directory listings: copy to paste

Answer engines cite third-party pages more than vendor pages, so being listed on the directories they read matters more than another page of our own. Everything below is ready to paste; the submitting is a human job (accounts, screenshots, verification emails).

## Where to list, in order of value

1. **G2** (g2.com/products/new): category "Presentation Software" and "AI Writing Assistant". Needs a company email and, for the badge, ten reviews. Ask the Starter subscribers first.
2. **Capterra / GetApp / Software Advice** (one form covers all three): category "Presentation Software".
3. **Product Hunt**: launch as "PitchBoost: upload a PowerPoint, get it back redesigned in your brand". Tuesday or Wednesday, 00:01 PT.
4. **AlternativeTo**: list as an alternative to Gamma, Beautiful.ai, Pitch, Canva, Tome. This is the page Perplexity cites for "alternatives to" questions.
5. **There's An AI For That**, **Futurepedia**, **SaaSHub**, **Toolify**: free listings, low effort, indexed by every assistant.
6. The "Gamma alternatives" listicles (eesel.ai, flowith.io, dynalord.com, aisotools.com all publish one): a short email offering the spec table from our comparison page usually gets a mention.
